## Visual direction

- The kit keeps IncomeNow's cool-white, charcoal and emerald system, with restrained Resumi blue used as a product accent.
- The original cover was generated specifically for this idea and contains abstract resume layouts, a laptop/phone composition and a score motif without third-party logos, people, currency or outcome claims.
- No owner-supplied Resumi brand asset is exposed in the public app. Product-logo and mockup rights remain an owner decision before any future rebrand or redistribution use.

## Evidence and limitations

- Live evidence is limited to the public Resumi site and synthetic inputs. No account, payment, real applicant data or production configuration was created.
- Observed behavior is separated from source-inspected, locally build-verified, disabled and future behavior throughout the kit.
- Resume-quality scoring is deterministic guidance, not ATS certification. Interview, employment, compatibility and revenue outcomes are not promised.
- Paid plans are described as disabled. The kit does not invent prices, renewal, trial duration or commercial terms.
- The product would process personal resume data. Production use requires owner-approved privacy terms, retention/deletion rules, security review, backups/recovery and customer-specific acceptance.
