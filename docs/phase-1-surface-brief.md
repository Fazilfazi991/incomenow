# Phase 1 member discovery surface

Mode: Operate. Members need to scan, narrow, inspect, save, and recover opportunities without losing their place.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm blueprint library where practical evidence and next actions outrank promotional framing. It refuses the generic analytics-dashboard arrangement and preserves the approved Stitch composition.

OWN-WORLD: Forest navigation anchors, pale sage working canvas, white structural cards, fine botanical borders, Manrope headings, Inter utility copy, compact metadata pills, and schematic previews derived from the supplied Editorial Venture Discovery system.

STORY: Members understand what an idea is, narrow the library, inspect the customer problem and implementation material, save a shortlist, and return to it. Sample and unavailable states remain explicit.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop opens with a fixed 228px rail and an editorial library header beside immediate search, sort, filters, and the first card row. Mobile opens with a compact brand bar, controls, and the first complete idea card, with bottom navigation always reachable.

FORM: Brief-pinned approved Stitch references; no concept seed applies because the user supplied the exact visual authority and requested preservation. Signature interaction: one bookmark state travels visibly from library to detail to shortlist, including reversible removal.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
