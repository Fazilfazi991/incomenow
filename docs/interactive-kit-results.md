# Pergola interactive-kit implementation results

Recorded: 21 September 2026.

## Outcome

The authorised Pergola URL is now an interactive business-kit hub. Members choose among seven outcome-focused activity cards and open one focused section at a time; the former long stack of complete sections is no longer rendered below the hub.

## Delivered

- Validated `?section=` state for opportunity, demo, software, setup, customers, sales, and delivery.
- Browser Back, direct section URLs, refresh-safe selected sections, Back to kit, a compact native selector, and full-load redirects for the former `#section-*` anchors.
- Direct hub actions for the external synthetic-data demo and entitlement-checked source ZIP.
- A meaningful-progress continuation derived only from existing saved checklist tasks or notes; no seven-activity completion percentage or decorative persistence.
- Customer/problem/offer opportunity cards and a proposed-model flow without demand, income, or result claims.
- A truthful demo module map based on inspected navigation, with synthetic/reset and untested-action boundaries.
- Protected-source and customisation views that preserve the offline/UAT boundary and show the missing setup guide honestly.
- A customer-research activity with real interview prompts and an explicit unavailable prospect-sheet state—no fictional companies or count.
- Editable email, discovery-call, and follow-up templates with placeholders, local draft state, functioning clipboard controls, and literal “copy is not send” feedback.
- Delivery guidance that reuses the one existing personal checklist. Genuine saved stage completion receives restrained treatment; hub visits and resource actions never alter it.
- Shared press/state/entry/confirmation motion tokens, reduced-motion suppression, focus restoration, keyboard controls, and natural page scrolling.

## Access and data boundaries

Authentication, starter/full access decisions, ownership, one-project limits, plan versions, tasks, notes, pause state, and the protected download handler are unchanged. Locked accounts still receive the safe preview and no kit sections. No migration or new progress store was added.
