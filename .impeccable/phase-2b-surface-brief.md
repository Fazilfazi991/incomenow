# Phase 2B personal workspace surfaces

Mode: Operate. Members need to keep a shortlist, begin one real project, and make steady progress without confusing sample material with persisted work.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm, accountable project desk where the next useful action is obvious and saved work feels durable. It extends the approved Stitch member world without turning into a generic task dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Forest navigation anchors, pale sage working canvas, white structural surfaces, fine botanical borders, Manrope headings, Inter utility copy, restrained progress bars, and the exact Saved Ideas, My Projects, and Project Workspace compositions supplied in the Stitch references.

STORY: Members save ideas into an account shortlist, promote one idea into a version-pinned project, work required checklist steps in order, keep a private note per stage, and pause or resume without losing history. Empty, paused, complete, saving, failure, and stale-note states explain what happened and what to do next.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop keeps the 228px member rail while Saved and Projects expose filters plus the first meaningful row; Workspace opens on project identity, honest derived progress, stage navigation, current checklist, notes, and stage resources. Mobile keeps the compact brand bar and reachable bottom navigation while stacking controls in task order.

FORM: Brief-pinned approved Stitch references; no concept seed applies because the user supplied the exact visual authority and requested preservation. Signature interaction: a saved idea becomes a durable project whose stage, checklist, progress, pause state, and note survive navigation and a second sign-in.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with bounded responsive screenshots, the detector, an independent finish review and verdict, updated DESIGN.md, and evidence-backed Phase 2B results.
