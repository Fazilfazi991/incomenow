# IncomeNow interface-system pointer

`DESIGN.md` at the repository root is the canonical IncomeNow design system. This file exists only so interface-design tooling has a stable discovery path; it must not become a second source of tokens, component rules, or brand decisions.

For public homepage work, use the canonical sections covering:

- the Modern Opportunity Workspace north star;
- cool-white and charcoal surfaces, emerald action, and controlled cyan/blue/violet illustration accents;
- the approved 8-second hero motion graphic, its poster fallback, and the selectable process explanation;
- modern public cover art with every claim and label kept in live HTML;
- the explicit retirement of the former warm, rustic, printed-kit direction.

For Pergola member-kit work, continue to use the canonical sections covering:

- scoped activity accents with emerald retained as the action voice;
- the responsive seven-card hub and one-activity-at-a-time section pattern;
- the Kit-Is-Not-Progress rule;
- shared press, state, enter, acknowledgement, and reduced-motion behavior;
- protected-resource truth, unavailable states, and the separate personal checklist.

If this pointer and `DESIGN.md` ever differ, `DESIGN.md` wins. Other projects may inherit the accessibility and interaction-quality process, but not IncomeNow's palette, typography, or Pergola-specific visual theme.
