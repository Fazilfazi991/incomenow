Pergola Quotation & Follow-up CRM

A bespoke pipeline and estimation workspace designed for timber and aluminium pergola contractors managing rapid multi-material quotation, site survey logs, and automated customer follow-ups.

Overview Demo Preview Action Plan Resources Finding Customers Updates & Notes

The Opportunity

Analysis & Model

This example explores a tailored CRM for pergola and timber shelter contractors seeking an authoritative, frictionless method to manage complex site inquiries, calculate multi-option proposals, and automate subsequent follow-up touches. Members should confirm this specific workflow with regional contractors prior to deploying bespoke instances.

Identified Operational Friction

Outdoor carpentry and pergola contractors lose lucrative contracts because manual, bespoke material quoting takes days. Unsystematic follow-ups allow warm homeowner leads to cool off or default to general contractors.

verified_user
Validation Requirement

Conduct structured discovery calls with at least 5 local builders or awning installers before writing custom code. Verify their current toolset, average proposal cycle time, and willingness to pay an implementation fee.

Recommended Engagement Model

One-Off Setup Fee + Monthly Managed Retainer

Typical packaging: £1,200 – £2,500 initial system adaptation and onboarding + £120/month for hosting, updates, and SMS transactional balance.

Who to Approach and What to Discuss

Outreach Playbook
target Target Buyers to Investigate
  • Pergola manufacturers and local timber framers
  • Bespoke outdoor carpentry contractors
  • Veranda, canopy, & motorized awning installers
  • High-end residential landscape design & build studios
co_present Suggested Demonstration Focus

Walk through the speed of generating a multi-option pergola quote on-site via tablet. Highlight how automated SMS follow-ups on Day 3 and Day 7 prevent high-value (£8k–£20k) inquiries from slipping through the cracks.

Example Discovery Questions
Q1 “How do you currently track quotes that haven’t received a response after the initial site survey?”
Q2 “How does your team know which customer needs a follow-up or confirmation today?”
Q3 “What exact dimensional and material information must move from the site surveyor to your carpentry workshop?”
lightbulb Core Rule: Confirm the contractor’s existing closing process before pitching software. A system can streamline a functioning sales cycle, but cannot rescue an undefined offer.
