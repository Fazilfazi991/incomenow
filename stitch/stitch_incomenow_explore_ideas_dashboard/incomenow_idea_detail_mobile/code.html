The Opportunity

High-end patio pergolas cost between £6,000 and £25,000. Most local fabricators handle lead follow-ups via WhatsApp and scattered email threads, leaking upwards of 30% of warm prospects who never receive a formal visual quote.

Finding Customers

Who to approach first

Target independent companies advertising on Instagram with captions like "DM for quote" or "Summer bookings filling up fast".

3 High-Yield Discovery Questions
Q1:

"How many homeowners ask for a rough quote each week vs how many you actually visit?"

Q2:

"When you send an estimate, how often do you have time to follow up with them twice?"

"What does a lost £10k installation contract cost your business in gross margin?"
