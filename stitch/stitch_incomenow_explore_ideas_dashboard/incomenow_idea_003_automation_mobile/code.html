Workflow Architecture

Illustrative workflow — no live external tools connected.

1
Event Trigger

Quotation Marked "Sent"

New quote status changes in the CRM, accounting tool, or tracker.

2
Date Calculation

Set Target Follow-up Date

System records `FollowUpDue = SentDate + 3 business days`.

3
Cron Scheduler

Scheduled Daily Scan

Runs every weekday morning at 09:00 AM to fetch due items.

4
Conditional Split Decision

Status Verification

Status == "Pending" Proceeds to Step 5
Accepted / Declined / Closed Skip & Exit
5
Action Dispatch

Create Internal Task

Creates task assigned to original quote author with link.

6
Audit Record

Activity Logging

Stamp quotation record with `FollowUpTaskCreated = TRUE` timestamp.

shield

Idempotency guard: Key off the combination of Quotation ID + Stage to strictly prevent duplicate task generation during retries.

Configure, Test & Hand Over

Document who writes proposals, how client changes are received, where quotes reside, and when staff usually give up follow-ups.
Lock in whether records stem from HubSpot, Zoho, Google Sheets, or custom CRM. Specify the follow-up window (e.g. +3 business days vs calendar days).
Implement schedule triggers, query filtering expressions, payload transformations, and task destination assignments in the chosen iPaaS.

Verify system stability across all edge scenarios prior to real deployment:

Shadow 2 sales reps for 5 business days. Gather feedback on task timing, title clarity, and review action speed.
Hand over credential tokens, document connection renewal timelines, and provide an SLA scope for future field additions.

Finding Customers

Ideal Client Profile

Focus exclusively on boutique service operators sending bespoke quotes where deal size warrants systematic attention:

Design & Dev Agencies Commercial Contractors IT Consultancies Corporate Event Planners

5 Diagnostic Interview Questions

1 "How many bespoke quotations or estimates does your firm distribute each month?"
2 "How do you currently find out that a sent quote hasn't received any response after 3 days?"
3 "Do your team members have a standardized task board for deals, or is it kept in memory/email?"
4 "If a potential client accepts or declines via phone or WhatsApp, where does that get recorded?"
5 "What tools do your sales people check first thing every morning for their daily priorities?"

Bounded Project Offer

Offer a flat-rate implementation: "Setup of 1 automated follow-up reminder pipeline connecting your existing CRM to task manager, with 1 week of live QA."

warning

Scope boundary warning: Never agree to build automated client-facing SMS or email sequences under this package. Internal tasks only.
